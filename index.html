🖨️ Brother DCP-T820DW Printing Guide (MP Tray & Best Quality)

Why were MP Tray and Paper options missing in the browser preview? Web browsers show a simplified preview. To get the exact Brother driver window you get in Microsoft Word, follow these simple steps:
1
Press Ctrl + Shift + P (Windows System Print Dialog)
When you click "Print Photos", instead of the standard browser dialog, press Ctrl + Shift + P (or scroll to the bottom of the Chrome preview and click "Print using system dialog...").
2
Select Brother DCP-T820DW → Click "Preferences" or "Properties"
This opens the native Brother printer driver control panel with all hardware options.
3
Paper Source → Select "MP Tray" (Manual Feed Slot)
The MP Tray is the rear slot on your Brother DCP-T820DW. It feeds thick photo paper (up to 300 gsm) straight without bending or jamming.
4
Media Type → Select "Photo Paper (Glossy)" or "Brother BP71"
Tells the printer to dispense high-density photo ink for brilliant, vivid colors.
5
Print Quality → Select "Photo" or "Best"
Prints at maximum resolution (1200 × 6000 dpi) for lab-quality crispness.
★
Or Use "Export for Microsoft Word"
Click the blue "Export for Microsoft Word (.doc)" button in PhotoPrint Studio. It downloads an exact A4 layout. Open it in Word, hit Ctrl + P, and print using the exact Word menu you already know!
